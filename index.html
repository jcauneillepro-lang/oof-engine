OOF · Section 01
Philosophy
Section 01 · Philosophy

Why this system exists.

Three convictions that shaped the language. They are the test every component must pass.

01 · WHY

Form is the rate-limiter on insight.

Every HR leader has decisions waiting on a deck. The deck is rarely the hard part — the thinking is. The system removes the design-and-format tax so the thinking gets through.

Inspired by Dieter Rams: good design is as little design as possible — but never less.

02 · HOW

One language, ruthlessly applied.

Navy ink. Lime accent. Cyan, magenta, orange, violet for hierarchy. Inter Tight for display, Inter for body. 57 components, all composable.

The constraint is the feature: a brief in v23 is recognisable across the company in two seconds.

03 · FOR WHOM

The next generation of HR leadership.

Built for the HR professionals who will lead businesses, not just functions. Briefs that hold their own at the ELT, the Board, and the off-site.

Source-honest. Evidence-cited. Verdict-forward.

OOF · Section 04
The Mark
Section 04 · The Mark

The wordmark is the mark.

No symbol. No logomark. The wordmark in Inter Tight 900 is the OOF identity in full. This is a deliberate constraint, not a missing piece.

OOF. Engine

Why no symbol.

Logos demand attention; words carry meaning. The OOF identity earns its presence through what it says, not how it sits in a frame. When you need an OOF stamp on a brief, use the wordmark — it works at any size, in any colour, on any background.

Problem · Insight · Resolution

PROBLEM

Critical-role turnover up 12% YoY while headcount grew 9%.

NACO is hitting growth targets but losing the people who matter — US Business Excellence voluntary attrition reached 37.7%, the highest in any function. Successor bench at N-1 is thin in 4 of 6 territories.

INSIGHT

The losses cluster in three roles that map to three external forces.

V&A bleed tracks IRA pricing pressure. MSL demand outstrips PharmD supply. Field force shrinkage forces hybrid rep-MSL profiles that don't yet exist in the org. The problem is structural, not cultural.

RESOLUTION

Buy / Bind / Build — €3.4M, decision by Jun 15.

Open the V&A req at +15% premium. 3-year retention LTI for top 12 V&A + 8 MSL Oncology. Marketing Academy cohort 2 with explicit RWE storytelling track. Outcome: critical-role voluntary turnover → 8–10% by Q4 26.

<

Three stacked bands force a clear narrative arc. The HR brief equivalent of the McKinsey pyramid — problem on top, insight middle, resolution bottom.

30

Three-act arc

Act I · Setup

The model that worked.

For two decades, NACO ran on scale field deployment. Reps + MSL + V&A in fixed proportions. Hire the seats, fill the territories, repeat. The system rewarded headcount discipline.

Act II · Conflict

The ground moved.

IRA priced launches differently. HCP access collapsed. AI compressed analytics. The reps the system optimised for are no longer the reps the market values. Top performers leave first because they see it first.

Act III · Resolution

Specialise. Embed. Compound.

Smaller, deeper field force. Account-based motion. AI co-pilot as table stakes. Hire for hybridity, not depth. Decide which lens — architectural, federated, transformative — and design backward.

three-act

Setup · Conflict · Resolution as three vertical columns with chevron flow. For change-management decks and transformation narratives.

Approve ACCELERATE before Jun 15 — €3.4M unlocks the workforce reshape NACO needs to be ready for 2030.
